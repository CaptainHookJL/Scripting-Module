Exit code: 0
$ ./either_test.sh /dev/null

empty.bin passes -f and -r but fails -s. Checking -f first with && short-circuits checks for an unsuitable object, preventing directories/devices from being treated as regular input. /dev/null is a character device, neither a directory nor a regular file. The final guard uses separate messages and exit codes: 2 not a regular file, 3 unreadable, 4 empty.
